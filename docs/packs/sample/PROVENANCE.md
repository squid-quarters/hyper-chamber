# Synthetic authoring example

Squid Quarters created this sample for Hyper Chamber on 16 November 2026.
It contains no game footage, game characters or tutorial excerpts. Demo Arena,
its marker and the `DEMO` notation are fictional file-format examples.

The two original SVG frames in `sources/` were rendered with ImageMagick, then
encoded as a four-second H.264 MP4 with ffmpeg. The JPEG is the first marker frame.
The recording is silent and visibly says “Demo footage — replace with your
recording” and “Not gameplay.”

The permission in COPYING covers this sample only. You may reuse and modify it.
Replace the recording, annotations and game profile before treating a fork as a
real fighting-game reference. Its verification status intentionally remains draft.
