# Create Reference Packs

Put your short replays, inputs and execution notes into a collection you can
import or share. Keep the workspace folder so you can edit the pack later;
your practice notes stay separately in your library.

**Requires the 1.1.0 development build.** Published installers are still 1.0.0.

## Use the Desktop Editor

[Make Your First Pack](first-pack.md): choose a trimmed clip, capture key moments,
add inputs and preview the result. The app includes the tools you need.

## Work with an AI Agent

[Install the pack-authoring skill](ai.md) and give the agent your recordings or
tutorial source. Review its inputs, timings and attribution before exporting.

## Try the Example

Download the [sample workspace](https://squid-quarters.github.io/hyper-chamber/create-packs/downloads/sample-workspace.zip)
and open the extracted folder in the creator, or [import the sample pack](https://squid-quarters.github.io/hyper-chamber/create-packs/downloads/sample.hcpack).
Its four-second recording is labeled **Not gameplay**.

For your own collection, start a new workspace or fork the sample to get
independent IDs. Replace its media, profile and annotations. The sample has its
own [reuse permission](sample/COPYING) and [source record](sample/PROVENANCE.md).
