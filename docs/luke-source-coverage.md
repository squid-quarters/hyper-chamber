## Interpretation and limits

Every entry is `source-reviewed` with `gameVersion: null`. Actions, buttons, starting states, resource use and selected moments were checked against footage and its input display. Captions are supporting evidence, not a guarantee of current-patch execution. Frame-advantage numbers printed in the footage are dated source observations; no universal reversal safety, guaranteed pressure or matchup coverage is inferred. Notes identify the prerequisite knockdown when a setup starts after it.

Flash Knuckle strength, Perfect release, and airborne short hold are separate execution requirements. This source supplies grounded confirms, corner juggles, conditional pressure and DI states; no anti-air category is invented.

## Media preparation

Full 1280×720 frames preserve HUD, inputs and overlays. The canonical ingestion helper prepares short silent H.264 clips, selected JPEGs and WebVTT action descriptions on a zero-based presentation clock. Both boundaries and every selected moment are visually reviewed; mechanical audits cover decoding, duration, image integrity and description timing. Originals and review boards stay outside Git. `content/media-luke.json` records private-development provenance; public redistribution is not assessed.
