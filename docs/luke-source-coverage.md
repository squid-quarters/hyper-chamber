## Practical coverage

Normal light, low, medium and heavy starters now have target-combo, regular/Perfect Flash Knuckle and Uppercut choices. The library includes Counter-Hit medium links, proximity-dependent Punish-Counter routes, actual Drive-Reversal and blocked OD-Uppercut punishes, a crouching-medium-kick whiff punish, DI counter armor/crumple and corner wall-splat, near-corner air-Knuckle juggles, observed carry, low OD DDT, Fatal Shot, double-Blast, meterless and OD finishers, Level 1/2/3 resources and two observed Burnout cash-outs. Luke has no character stock system; Super stocks refer to the gauge, and opaque legacy IDs containing “stock” do not assert a Luke stock mechanic.

Setup families include regular-light-Knuckle +36 dash/Rush guard, throw, shimmy, low and overhead; Perfect-light/heavy-ender Avenger and Rush decisions; corner light/heavy-Uppercut medium/jab/standing-medium timing whiffs; target-combo dash/walk throw, shimmy and heavy strike; heavy-Uppercut dash throw/shimmy/Counter-Hit medium; Level 2 walk decisions; DDT walk decisions; shortened Perfect-light ender dash/walk decisions; Avenger-ender dash/walk and intentional whiffed-Avenger decisions; guarded raw-Rush medium delay throw, shimmy and Counter-Hit medium; forward-throw Rush guard; corner forward-throw walk throw/shimmy; back-throw heavy-kick wake-up conversion; and held-heavy guard/parry responses. Different prerequisite knockdowns remain explicit rather than silently interchangeable.

Responses include actual OD-Uppercut and Drive-Reversal guards into punishes, a Drive-Parry/Level-1 demonstration after a timing whiff, two specific recorded jump-in reversal baits and an airborne Counter-Hit conversion. The 2023 bait footage blocks Juri OD Tensenrin and Ryu OD Shoryuken; it does not establish a universal safe-jump guarantee. The airborne route actually uses jump medium punch, a held medium air Flash Knuckle and medium Uppercut against Blanka.

## Source interpretation and limits

Every addition is `source-reviewed` with `gameVersion: null`. No current-patch in-game execution is claimed. Opponent behavior, Counter Hit/Punish Counter, corner distance, required Super and Perfect-release timing remain tied to the performed take. Printed “any” and alternative strengths are not evidence that every listed branch was executed.

The February full guide's regular-light Knuckle knockdown is +32; the June update compares it with +36 and demonstrates an immediate Avenger follow-up whiffing. The old immediate-Avenger timing is excluded. The June beginner's +36 decisions are retained separately. Other source-specific timings remain dated demonstrations without a current-patch validity guarantee.

Printed `+F` values describe remaining advantage, not a prescribed wait. Only explicit performed/captioned delays appear as qualitative delay actions. Intentional frame kills say `(whiff)` in compact notation. Setup inputs/resources begin after the named prerequisite; earlier combo, Drive and Super costs are separate. Some excerpts retain the prerequisite's final recovery to show the entire first timing action.

Original input history resolves several shorthand gaps: the full guide's heavy family at 7:55–10:42 starts with crouching heavy punch despite an abbreviated overlay, while the later family starts standing heavy. The standing-medium timing-whiff jab sequence includes an actual `cr.LK` before its two jabs. The medium/light-kick OD walk route uses OD Flash Knuckle, then walks into heavy Uppercut; it is not OD Uppercut. The corner blocked-Uppercut air conversion finishes with light Uppercut. A single Perfect-medium command can produce two hits and is not automatically transcribed as two commands. All three actual jabs in the June-update Level-2 confirm are retained.

`Corner` describes the excerpt's starting situation. Routes beginning away from the wall use `Midscreen`; `Corner carry` requires visible travel. Super use alone does not imply `Cash-out`: the two new tagged examples visibly reach EMPTY/Burnout. Nominal action costs are not presented as minimum starting-gauge calculations where regeneration occurs. Held-heavy guard/parry and wake-up pressure do not claim gapless strings or universal reversal safety.
