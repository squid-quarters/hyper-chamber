## Meter and response limits

Normal heavy Crusher is meterless. Mine-enhanced OD Crusher spends two Drive bars even though it changes camera; that animation is not a Super. Heavy Shadow Rise followed by the separate two-kick OD air follow-up costs another two Drive bars. The full OD-Crusher/OD-air conversions therefore spend four Drive, in addition to whichever Super is actually performed. Prerequisite actions outside the loop are excluded from that loop’s displayed cost.

The Critical Art example shows the source CA indicator in regenerative training settings. It requires CA availability and three Super bars; a visible low-health threshold is not inferred from that HUD. Other Super1 and ordinary Super3 routes remain separate. The two Rush-cancel low cash-out spends six Drive and enters Burnout.

The defensive clips specifically demonstrate Ryu’s OD uppercut or Drive Reversal. Their guard/punish timing does not establish universal reversal safety. Counter-hit and punish-counter links retain their recorded restrictions. Corner cards begin at the corner; midscreen starting situations remain separate.
