## Interpretation and limits

All additions are source-reviewed with `gameVersion: null`; current-patch execution has not been tested in-game. Both source recordings are from 2023. No frame, damage, safety or matchup statement is promoted to a current-patch guarantee. Knockdown prerequisites are separate from the follow-up sequence and its costs.

The four corner doll branches require the demonstrated corner knockdown, a remaining doll, the recorded Drive resources, three Super stocks and low health for the shown Critical Art variant. The reversal branch visibly guards Jamie’s OD reversal before punishing; it is not an unguarded evasion. The grab/mash label describes one recorded interaction, not coverage of every throw or mash timing.

The Scorp hop prerequisite is `j.HK > cr.HP > 2PP > P > 3HP`. The hit branch uses back medium kick, crouching jab and light Electricity. The throw branch shows blocked back medium kick, forward walk and a throw. Its original input display omits the throw chord, so the recipe says `Throw` and explicitly leaves exact throw-button timing unverified.

Inputs and special strengths follow the visible original glyphs. The low-light Rush route ends in medium Electricity; the shorter triple-jab and forward-medium routes use light Electricity. The stun doll is heavy placement, while all four corner reset dolls use light placement. `63214K` means Backstep Rolling Attack.

Source +F captions describe remaining advantage, not a prescribed wait. The raw-Rush/two-cancel-Rush cash-out visibly spends through the gauge and ends in burnout. Charge directions remain character-relative. The air-throw sample is tagged midscreen because that is where the selected demonstration actually begins, despite nearby source discussion of corner use.

Every new annotated moment and both prepared loop boundaries have been visually inspected. Full-frame silent H264 clips retain HUD/input displays, use zero-based playback clocks, and carry canonical timed descriptions. Brief original crossfades remain where removing them would cut the first action. Media publication wording remains pending repository-level clarification; public redistribution has not been assessed.
