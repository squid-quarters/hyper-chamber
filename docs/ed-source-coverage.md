## Interpretation and limits

The medium-Blitz +39, target-chain heavy-Blitz +42, crouching-medium-pair heavy-Blitz +40 and medium-Uppercut +39 prerequisites remain separate. Setup loops name the exact prerequisite and begin in its recovery context before the first authored action. `+F` reports remaining advantage; it never becomes a wait count. Intentional frame-kill whiffs appear in compact inputs. Only explicit delayed-throw examples contain a nonnumeric delay instruction.

The corner Spark uses the visible **236LP** command. The source prints later throw/shimmy/medium options after block, but only the blocked Spark is executed in those takes. The hit branches are distinct demonstrations with heavy punch, late Chaser and heavy Uppercut. Ed’s crouching-medium-kick animation is a body punch and the HUD calls it High; it is not presented as a low confirm.

The OD-Flicker routes use heavy Flicker, **forward Kill Rush and early 6P Kill Switch Break**, distinct from the late Chaser follow-up. The safe-jump response shows **j.HP followed by landing guard against Ryu OD uppercut**; other reversals and Supers are not certified.

The Cannon source caption `LSA2` agrees with its live **cyan light-punch activation**, followed by a neutral release. The recipe preserves `SA2 (LP)`. Another Cannon strength or an additional held-back speed control is not demonstrated. Cannon remains active during both Uppercuts and the forward-heavy pickup.
