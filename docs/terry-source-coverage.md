## Interpretation and limits

All entries are source-reviewed with `gameVersion: null`; current-patch execution has not been tested in-game. The source’s age and balance context remain visible beside every reference. No damage, frame advantage, safety or matchup claim is promoted to a current-patch guarantee. Conditions distinguish normal hit, Counter Hit, Punish Counter, blocked-DI wall splat and corner position. Pressure sequences are not represented as guaranteed combos.

Brackets mean holding a direction before releasing the listed input. Inputs are character-relative; mirror after a side switch. Every annotated key moment and both clip boundaries are visually inspected. The canonical ingestion helper encodes full-frame silent clips, resets their playback clock and writes timed descriptions.

This collection deliberately omits uncertain extractions, unreviewed alternatives and any claim to exhaustive category coverage. Longer teaching remains linked externally. Media remains private development material; public redistribution has not been assessed.

The full Geyser cash-out starts midscreen and carries Ryu toward the corner. It does not require an initial wall position.
