Burnout examples begin with guarded actions before a later counter hit or throw. They are pressure interactions, not one continuous combo and not universal interruption guarantees. Their live yellow inputs establish medium Charge/Tackle; OD Power Wave uses two punches, not kicks. The Akuma examples demonstrate the jump action and grounded guard of the actual OD uppercut; no universal reversal safety or later punish is prescribed.

Drive Impact crumple, normal blocked-Impact wall splat and OD-uppercut guard punishes retain their actual conditions. The corner full Geyser route records both raw Rushes, OD Wave, the Rush cancel and both Geyser continuations. Twin Geyser spends Drive and enters Burnout; Triple Geyser spends the third Super stock.
