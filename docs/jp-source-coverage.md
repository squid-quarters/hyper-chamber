## Interpretation and limits

Every entry is `source-reviewed` with `gameVersion: null`. Actions, buttons, starting states, resource use and selected moments were checked against footage and its input display. Captions are supporting evidence, not a guarantee of current-patch execution. Frame-advantage numbers printed in the footage are dated source observations; no universal reversal safety, guaranteed pressure or matchup coverage is inferred. Notes identify the prerequisite knockdown when a setup starts after it.

The guide often prints a post-launch recipe while the performed take also includes an earlier starter. This collection authors the actions actually shown. Medium and heavy Stribog, Torbalan projectiles, Triglav placements, and Departure portal placement are kept distinct. Most simple confirms stop before the source’s next portal-placement action. The initial selection omits uncertain multi-portal and Level-2 long routes rather than guessing them.

## Media preparation

Full 1280×720 frames preserve HUD, inputs and overlays. The canonical ingestion helper prepares short silent H.264 clips, selected JPEGs and WebVTT action descriptions on a zero-based presentation clock. Both boundaries and every selected moment are visually reviewed; mechanical audits cover decoding, duration, image integrity and description timing. Originals and review boards stay outside Git. `content/media-jp.json` records private-development provenance; public redistribution is not assessed.
