# Street Fighter 6 roster coverage

## What was expanded

The 26 expanded libraries add distinct practical demonstrations, including documented punish routes, corner carry, knockdown/throw trees, whiffed frame kills, counter-hit and Punish-Counter branches, resource choices, anti-air and matchup responses where corroborated footage is available. Counts vary with the reviewed source material and the number of useful demonstrated decisions. Different starting states, costs or defensive responses can justify separate references; a longer replay or an arbitrary prefix does not by itself justify another card.

All selected additions passed an independent source/media review. Existing reference IDs and learner-data keys are retained. Three execution corrections revise the affected baseline entries: Lily's first medium Buster uses punch, and two Terry routes use OD Charge. JP's pre-existing medium-projectile route has a title-only correction; its inputs were already medium. Their character pages explain the evidence. Remaining original entries and their media are preserved.

## Review and limits

These are source-reviewed demonstrations, not in-game verification on the current patch. Each reference retains its source creator, original video, publication date and interval. Character-specific charge, stocks, stance, install, medal, drink, poison and prerequisite knockdowns belong beside the relevant reference. Counter hits, trades, blocked pressure, armor damage and true guards are distinguished.

The expanded libraries have machine-readable [source ledgers](source-ledgers/) that separate selected/reused, excluded and unprocessed footage. Discovery surveys and metadata scouting do not mean every execution route was extracted. Availability of more videos is recorded honestly; a small selected subset is not evidence that no more useful footage exists. Unprocessed intervals and character-specific anti-air, matchup or optimization gaps remain documented.

Every bundled reference has a short silent clip, selected key moments and timed action descriptions. All new selected images and actual first/last decoded frames were visually reviewed; independent reviews check recipes, resource/state requirements, source provenance, timing and risky HUD/input details. Mechanical audits check full clip decoding, presentation-clock reset, duration, canonical descriptions, valid images and missing/orphaned assets. Deterministic checks and browser testing are separate from source review. None of these establishes present-patch safety, damage, universal matchup coverage or public redistribution rights.

GitHub repository visibility is public. Asset manifests retain their private-development status; an open-source license and public media redistribution policy have not been chosen. Newly prepared excerpts remain local pending the publication decision. Originals and extraction workfiles remain outside Git. Long lessons stay external through SF6 Lab and the credited source creators.
