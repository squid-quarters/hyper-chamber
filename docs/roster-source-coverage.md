# Street Fighter 6 roster coverage

Reviewed on 11 November 2026. Hyper Chamber has 727 selected references and 3310 annotated moments across all 32 released SF6 characters. The 26 newly added libraries are starting practice collections, not comprehensive character guides. Each row links to the exact selections, source intervals, execution conditions and omissions.

The roster includes Ingrid, Yasmine and Arjun, whose releases are confirmed in [Capcom's Ingrid announcement](https://www.capcomfrance.fr/ingrid-sf6-sortie/), [Yasmine release news](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1364780/view/1839676055894097) and [Arjun announcement](https://press.capcom.com/Street-Fighter-6-Welcomes-Arjun-to-the-Mat-on-October-13). Unreleased characters are excluded. Long-form strategy remains linked through [SF6 Lab](https://sf6-lab.net/en/).

## Review and limits

These are source-reviewed demonstrations from 40 recordings, not in-game verification on the current patch. Each reference retains the source creator, original video, publication date and interval. Character-specific charge, stocks, stance, install, medal, drink, poison and other conditions belong beside the relevant reference, rather than being assumed across a library.

Coverage follows reliable footage. A zero in the setup or response column is an explicit coverage gap, not a claim that the character lacks those options. Where a tutorial does not establish an important button, route condition or timing, the uncertain extraction is omitted. The per-character pages record more specific omissions. Additional oki and matchup material can be added through the same review process.

Every bundled reference has a prepared silent clip, selected key moments and timed action descriptions. Original recordings and extraction workfiles remain outside Git. Source review and prepared-media checks are separate; neither establishes present-patch safety, damage, universal matchup coverage or public redistribution rights. The repository remains private development material.
