# Hyper Chamber design

The current visual and interaction rules are maintained in [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md). The [original palette and Hourglass H exploration](design-system-exploration.md) remains as the adoption record.

## Earlier structure research

The library and practice flow were informed by viewed screens from [Zoom](https://mobbin.com/screens/8ac631df-2124-44da-8199-2346d67375ac), [Vimeo](https://mobbin.com/screens/61a592c7-dc5b-40ce-bfb2-51498a6b68dc), [Loom](https://mobbin.com/screens/5d0ce4ff-a6cc-4c5b-ac3c-fa78e2b1a59a) and [Arcade](https://mobbin.com/screens/d71b6ebd-e45f-48de-92f4-92642e4c699f). They supplied examples of searchable media libraries and filtering, not a replacement identity.

[Attio](https://mobbin.com/screens/b1d64fe7-82a9-42dd-9487-6385ac347cc6) and [incident.io](https://mobbin.com/screens/45365384-2ea7-4a55-bb83-2384579937f5) supplied examples of connected steps with attached information. Hyper Chamber's horizontal ordered-input adaptation remains a read-only reference. It has no authored input-to-frame mapping.

The 48-pixel Previous/Next targets follow the larger-target intent of [W3C target-size guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-enhanced.html).
