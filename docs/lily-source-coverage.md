## Interpretation and limits

Every addition is source-reviewed with `gameVersion: null`. No current-patch execution was tested in-game. The recording date is not a patch guarantee for frame advantage, damage, safety or matchups. Source +F captions describe advantage rather than an instruction to wait. Holds, delays and micro-walk distances follow the demonstrated action without invented frame counts.

Live inputs take precedence over printed shorthand: the near-corner light route uses standing lights, and raw-Rush jab conversions use crouching heavy punch. The three-Wind corner route explicitly requires three prepared stocks. Ordinary OD variants have no Wind stock; the held-OD-Wind punish gains Wind during its own route. The air-to-air routes use two j.MP target inputs in one jump, then Buster after landing.

The longer raw-Rush/two-cancel-Rush cash-out punishes an OD Shoryuken that misses Lily at the recorded spacing. The preceding blocked jab and whiffed crouching light kick are stated prerequisites. Other selected reversal routes visibly guard their recorded attack; none certifies universal reversal coverage. The cash-out uses normal-health Level 3, and its nominal seven-bar sequence enters burnout from the shown six-bar gauge.

The short-held-heavy-Wind take shows an ordinary parry, despite a caption advertising Perfect-Parry counterplay. The light-Wind counter-DI exchange absorbs recoverable damage with armor. The held-heavy-Wind take that loses to DI is excluded. These outcomes are described as performed.

Source frames retain HUD/input displays, clips are silent H264 with zero-based clocks, and descriptions match the selected moments.
