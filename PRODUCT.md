# Hyper Chamber

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Fighting-game players looking up a particular combo, setup or response while practicing. The initial owner mains Dhalsim; the reference libraries are shared product content, separate from each learner's notes and progress.

## Product Purpose

A quick visual compendium of individual demonstrations, ordered inputs, selected moments and short execution notes. Success means finding the wanted route and inspecting its replay during practice. Link to SF6 Lab for guides and lessons.

## Positioning

Each reference keeps its input sequence and short replay together, with selected frames for the moments a player needs to inspect repeatedly. The library is a navigable reference rather than a lesson sequence.

## Operating Context

Used in the desktop app or a browser beside a running fighting game, often in a short laptop window or on a phone connected to the same local library. Players switch between related routes, filter by a training goal and repeatedly pause, replay or inspect a short clip with optional source audio.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Street Fighter 6 has populated character libraries. Other fighting games, including tag fighters, are future interests; game definitions own notation and vocabulary. References retain stable IDs, source provenance and explicit verification limits. Saved entries, practice status and notes live in the local SQLite library. The Tauri desktop app bundles its server and can share the same library with phone and tablet browsers on the local network. Browser storage holds playback preferences and recovery drafts. Content packs remain separate from learner data; library controls provide imports, folder selection and database snapshots. There are no accounts, synchronization, gameplay detection or current-patch certification. See [scope and behavior](docs/product.md) for the full boundaries.

## Brand Commitments

The name pays homage to the Hyperbolic Time Chamber. Keep the Hourglass H identity and the adopted Chamber palette. The owner prefers a restrained interface with factual copy, conspicuous practice navigation, portraits beside character names and visual input notation. Remove slogans and garish active outlines.

## Evidence on Hand

Real reference catalogs, replay excerpts and selected source frames live in the repository. Preserve authored commands, constraints and media provenance. Do not invent gameplay results, timings or training progress. Character portraits are generated illustrations based on attributed high-resolution identity references; they are interface artwork, not source footage or in-game verification.

## Product Principles

- Keep the wanted reference quick to find and inspect.
- Put the input sequence before the replay, with extra information below.
- Keep shared content separate from personal practice data.
- Distinguish source review from in-game verification.
- Let real content determine the layout.
